
## My ISC² Certified in Cybersecurity (CC) Study Notes

Hey everyone,

I’m putting these notes together because I know how tough it is to prepare for the CC exam when you don’t have paid access to the official ISC² course materials. I created this repo **CC_Study_Notes** specifically for self-learners like me who are studying on a budget. Everything here is based on the **official ISC² CC Exam Outline** (the one valid until 31 August 2026). I’ve organized it in a clean, Obsidian-friendly way so you can jump straight in and start studying.

These aren’t just dry bullet points; I’ve written them the way I wish someone had explained things to me: clear, practical, and with the key things I actually remembered on exam day.

### Quick Exam Facts (What You Need to Know First)

- **Exam length**: 2 hours - **Number of questions**: 100–125 (Computerized Adaptive Testing) 
- **Passing score**: 700 out of 1000 
- **Format**: Mostly multiple-choice, some advanced question types 
- **No prerequisites**: Zero experience required; just solid basic IT knowledge helps 
- **Where to take it**: Pearson VUE centers (English is the main language, a few others available)
